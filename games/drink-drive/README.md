# DUI Racing Demo — Educational Warning Simulation

## ⚠️ **IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER**
**This is an educational simulation designed to demonstrate the dangers of drinking and driving. NEVER drink and drive in real life. This game is intended to raise awareness about the serious consequences of impaired driving.**

## 🎯 **Game Concept**

The DUI Racing Demo is an interactive educational tool that simulates how alcohol consumption impairs driving ability. Players experience firsthand how even small amounts of alcohol can dramatically affect their control, reaction time, and decision-making while driving.

### **Core Educational Message**
- **Alcohol impairs driving at ANY level** - even one drink affects performance
- **No amount of alcohol is safe when driving**
- **Real-world consequences are life-threatening** - this simulation shows only a fraction of the danger

## 🎮 **How It Works**

### **Pre-Race Selection**
Before starting the race, players choose how many beers to consume (0-10). The interface shows real-time previews of how alcohol will affect:
- **Speed**: Alcohol provides a false sense of confidence and speed
- **Control**: Steering becomes increasingly difficult and unpredictable
- **Vision**: Visual impairment increases with alcohol consumption

### **The Race Experience**
Players drive down a straight drag strip with four lanes, encountering:
- **Moving obstacles**: Cars, trucks, and boxes traveling in both directions
- **Side barriers**: Red barriers that cause crashes and speed reduction
- **Progressive impairment**: Effects worsen with higher alcohol levels

### **Impairment Effects by Alcohol Level**

| Beers | Speed Bonus | Control Penalty | Vision Impairment | Swerving |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|-------------------|----------|
| 0     | Normal      | Normal          | Clear             | None     |
| 1-3   | +10-35%     | -5 to -20%      | Slight blur       | Mild     |
| 4-6   | +50-90%     | -30 to -50%     | Moderate blur     | Noticeable |
| 7-10  | +110-200%   | -60 to -90%     | Heavy impairment  | Severe   |

## 🕹️ **Controls**
- **A/D** or **Arrow Keys**: Steer left/right
- **Space**: Accelerate
- **R**: Reset game

## 🚗 **Game Features**

### **Dynamic Obstacles**
- **Cars**: Green (same direction) vs Red (opposite direction)
- **Boxes**: Cargo that may have fallen from trucks
- **Trucks**: Large vehicles that are hardest to avoid

### **Realistic Physics**
- **Collision consequences**: 80% speed reduction, screen shake, car spin
- **Barrier crashes**: Significant speed penalty and crash counter
- **Progressive difficulty**: More alcohol = more unpredictable movement

### **Visual Impairment Effects**
- **Vignette effect**: Tunnel vision that worsens with alcohol
- **Blur simulation**: Screen becomes increasingly unclear
- **Screen shake**: Represents loss of focus and coordination

### **Audio Feedback**
- **Collision sounds**: Different tones for barriers vs obstacles
- **Victory fanfare**: Celebratory music upon race completion
- **Warning sounds**: Audio cues for dangerous situations

## 📊 **Results & Education**

### **Post-Race Analysis**
The game provides detailed statistics including:
- Race completion time
- Maximum and average speed
- Number of crashes and collisions
- Personalized educational message based on performance

### **Educational Messages**
Results screens provide tailored feedback:
- **0 beers**: Reinforcement of safe driving choices
- **Low alcohol, no crashes**: Warning that luck doesn't equal safety
- **Multiple crashes**: Clear demonstration of alcohol's dangers
- **High alcohol levels**: Severe warnings about potentially fatal consequences

## 🎓 **Learning Objectives**

### **Primary Goals**
1. **Demonstrate alcohol's immediate impact** on driving ability
2. **Show that any amount of alcohol impairs driving**
3. **Illustrate the unpredictable nature** of impaired driving
4. **Reinforce safe alternatives** to drinking and driving

### **Real-World Connections**
- **Legal consequences**: Fines, license suspension, jail time
- **Safety risks**: Injury or death to yourself and others
- **Social responsibility**: Impact on families and communities
- **Alternative solutions**: Designated drivers, ride-sharing, public transport
